# Style guide: EN (UK English) blog posts

Inherits `../02-STYLE-GUIDE-MASTER.md`. Source: dlvibe `STYLE-GUIDE-EN.md`, adapted.

## Reader
UK nationals relocating to or investing in Spain: retirees buying property, investors, British
entrepreneurs, Digital Nomad / Startup visa holders, UK SMEs, cross-border inheritance, remote
workers. Secondary: IFAs, tax advisers, foreign law firms.

## Rules
- UK spelling: organise, specialise, licence (noun), judgement, labour, programme.
- Post-Brexit framing where relevant (third-country national rules, 90/180 days, S1, ISAs, SIPPs,
  QROPS, HMRC residence tests). Don't write generic EU copy with "UK" swapped in.
- Headings and H1 in **sentence case** (6 posts use Title Case H1s). Title tags too.
- Banned (in addition to the master list): "discover", "genuinely", "honestly", "straightforward",
  "it is important to", "navigate the complexities", "65 years of experience".
- Tone: professional, precise, readable for non-lawyers, never chatty.
- Note: the old brief's "two line breaks after every full stop" is a WordPress-era formatting
  quirk. Don't apply it. Short paragraphs do the same job (DECISIONS D-6).
- Pipes in title tags: 13 EN posts. Replace with a colon.
