# Style guide: RU (Russian) blog posts

Inherits `../02-STYLE-GUIDE-MASTER.md`. Source: dlvibe `STYLE-GUIDE-RU.md`, adapted.

## Reader
Russian-speaking residents and buyers in Spain: non-lucrative residency, property, banking,
inheritance. 28 posts, median about 680 words, the thinnest locale. No blog support in 6 of 8
practice-area clusters.

## Rules
- **Terminology, always fix**: «вид на жительство без права на работу» (residencia no
  lucrativa). The calque «ненасыщенный вид на жительство» is wrong every time.
- Formal «Вы» (capitalised when addressing the reader directly, standard for legal copy).
- No «Золотая виза» as an available route (posts 12942 and 3459 mention it, so check the context).
- No «бесплатная консультация».
- **CTA contact (Mike, 2026-10-02): the firm's Russian-speaking agent, never Félix.** Use exactly what
  the RU service pages publish: «Свяжитесь с Olesia Davidova: o.davidova@delaguialuzon.com или
  +34 96 352 32 91» (`mailto:o.davidova@delaguialuzon.com`, `tel:+34963523291`), plus a link to
  `/ru/контакты/`. Other locales keep +34 963 74 16 57 / felix.delaguia@delaguialuzon.com.
- Sanctions context: don't suggest services for entities established in Russia or Belarus (EU
  restrictions on legal advisory services). The reader is an individual resident in Spain.
- Cyrillic slugs stay as they are (changing them needs redirects and Mike's approval).
- Open (DECISIONS D-5): banned openers and heading-capitalisation conventions for RU have no native
  sign-off yet. Use sentence case and avoid meta-textual openers («В этой статье мы расскажем…»).
- RU posts are the only ones showing a visible date (the RU template variant has the post-info date).
