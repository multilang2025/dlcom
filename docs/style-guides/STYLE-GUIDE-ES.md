# Style guide: ES (Spanish) blog posts

Inherits `../02-STYLE-GUIDE-MASTER.md`. Source: dlvibe `STYLE-GUIDE-ES.md`, adapted.

## Reader
Spanish residents and Spanish-speaking international clients (LatAm investors, Spaniards with
cross-border situations). ES is the root locale (`/blog/<slug>/`), but EN and FR are deeper.

## Rules
- Register: **usted** (open: "formal usted throughout or nuanced by audience", see DECISIONS D-4).
  Until decided, use usted and never mix usted and tú in one post.
- Headings in sentence case (Spanish norm: only the first word and proper nouns capitalised).
- Banned: "Descubre/Descubra", "es importante", "consulta gratuita", "sin coste", "65 años" (only
  the title of the 2025 anniversary film keeps it), "Golden Visa"/"visado de oro" as available.
- Use the official Spanish names of institutions and forms (Agencia Tributaria, Modelo 720, TIE),
  with no English glosses.
- Valencian regional law: cite DOGV, hisenda.gva.es, atv.gva.es. Use "Comunitat Valenciana" in
  legal context and "Comunidad Valenciana" in running text.
- Title tags must not carry emojis (two posts did on 2026-10-01: 27823, 23447).
