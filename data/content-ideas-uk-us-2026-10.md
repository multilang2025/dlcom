## 2. Verified facts that change the plan

- **UK inheritance tax: confirmed.** IHTA 1984 s.6A (Finance Act 2025 s.44), from 6 April 2025: UK resident for
  at least 10 of the previous 20 tax years. The tail is 3 years for 13 or fewer years of residence, rising one
  year per extra year to 10 years at 20 years. HMRC's list of IHT treaties has no Spain. Spanish succession
  tax and its Valencian bonuses for non-resident heirs are **not verified**: no figures in N-2 until they are.
- **Non-resident income tax (IRNR): corrected.** UK residents pay 24 % on rents, with no deductions (art. 25.1.a
  and 24.6; AEAT confirms for income accrued from 1 Jan 2021). EU/EEA residents pay 19 % and can deduct.
  **Capital gains on property are 19 % for every non-resident**, not 24 %, with 3 % withheld by the buyer.
- **Modelo 210 and Orden HAC/623/2026 (corrected):** the Orden does not create the annual return (grouping of
  rents has been annual since accruals of 2024). It moves deadlines: rents with an amount to pay are filed in the
  first 20 days of April of the following year, and imputed income from 1 April to 31 December, applying from
  accruals of 2026 (first filing April 2027). A new expenses annex applies from filings in 2027. The FR and RU
  posts changed this week already use this wording (checked 2026-10-06), so nothing needs fixing there.
- **US-Spain treaty: corrected.** The 2013 Protocol (signed in Madrid 14 January 2013) entered into force on
  **27 November 2019** (BOE-A-2019-15166). Social security payments fall under art. 20(1)(b) ("may be taxed" by
  the paying State, not exclusive). The Protocol did **not** change the savings clause (art. 1(3)) or the
  tie-breaker (art. 4(2)). Withholding: dividends 5 % (10 % holding) or 15 %, interest 0 %, royalties 0 %.
  The totalization agreement (signed 1986, in force 1 April 1988) covers contributions and benefits only, not
  income tax.
- **Valencian wealth tax: confirmed, with a limit that changes the angle.** Ley 5/2026, art. 19: exempt minimum
  **2 000 000 €**, in force 11 August 2026, applied to wealth held on 31 December 2026. It is for **residents of
  the Comunitat**. Non-residents get the state **700 000 €** (LIP art. 28 Tres). So UK and US owners do not
  get the 2 M€: N-5 must say so. ITSGF: 3 M€ threshold, still prorogued, filed 1 to 31 July, non-residents
  included (re-check before publishing). The RU post 24366 already states the 700 000 € for non-residents.
- **Modelo 720 and 721: confirmed.** 50 000 € per category, filed 1 January to 31 March, re-filed only after a
  20 000 € rise. Ley 5/2022 repealed the old 5 000 € per item and 150 % regime after CJEU C-788/19. Penalties are
  now LGT art. 198 (20 € per datum, minimum 300 €, maximum 20 000 €). DGT V0890-24: rights in a UK pension plan
  are not reportable on Modelo 720 before a contingency occurs. **Modelo 721 appears nowhere on the EN blog.**
- **UK pensions: partly unverified.** Private pensions: art. 17 UK-Spain treaty (taxed only in the residence
  State). **UK State Pension:** DGT V2460-21 applies art. 18(2) (taxed only in the UK, exempt with
  progression in Spain for a British national resident in Spain). **Unverified, do not state as fact:** the
  Spanish treatment of the UK 25 % tax-free lump sum, of SIPPs, and of ISA income (no AEAT or DGT statement).
- **Keyword clash:** move "non-resident income tax in spain" off 2175 before publishing N-1.

## 4. Rules for these posts

- **Spanish law and the Valencian angle first.** Say at the top of each post that it covers Spanish law,
  and recommend that readers coordinate with their home-country adviser for US or UK tax filing.
  This also serves the secondary audience of IFAs and foreign firms. This is a proposal (DECISIONS D-11)
  until Mike confirms it. Existing posts 20275 and 22034 already comment on US or UK tax.
- UK spelling for all EN posts, post-Brexit framing (STYLE-GUIDE-EN). US posts: UK spelling is kept for
  consistency with the site, with American terms explained in brackets on first use.
- Every figure, rate, threshold and treaty article goes through the fact-checking protocol, with inline
  official sources (BOE, AEAT, HMRC, IRS, SSA, EUR-Lex, DOGV). Anything unverified becomes a "contact us" CTA.
- TL;DR first, FAQ as H3, CTA before the FAQ, EN contact Félix (+34 963 74 16 57,
  felix.delaguia@delaguialuzon.com).
- Each new post is created as a **draft** and goes through `dl-blog-editor`, `dl-fact-checker` and
  `dl-qa-publisher`. Mike approves publishing.
- After publishing, run `python tools/related_posts_audit.py en`: Link Whisper sometimes assigns the wrong
  language to new posts.
