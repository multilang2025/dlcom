## What was wrong and is fixed
- 24% on the gain for non-EU sellers (TL;DR, body, table, FAQ "UK sellers", FAQPage schema). The rate is 19% for every
  non-resident (art. 25.1.f.3 TRLIRNR). 24% is the general rate for other income such as rent.
- Modelo 210 deadline: now the wording of art. 5.a Orden EHA/3316/2010 (three months once one month has passed from the
  transfer). HAC/623/2026 changed rents and imputed income deadlines, not this one.
- Modelo 211: one month from the transfer date (not "of the notarial deed"). Buyer gives the seller a copy.
- Plusvalia: calculation (art. 107.5 real gain if lower, art. 104.5 no gain no tax), 30 working days (art. 110.2.a), buyer is
  the substitute taxpayer when the seller is a non-resident individual (art. 106.2).
- Removed with no replacement (unsourced): Registrars statistic, "30 to 45 days", "0.5% to 1%" fees row, "six to twelve months"
  refund time, "nota simple within 30 days", "typically".
- Removed (wrong): "four-month deadline or lose the refund", "Modelo 720 for Spanish accounts after the sale", "sworn energy
  certificate" and "IBI receipts are legal preconditions".
- Added with sources: community debt certificate (LPH art. 9.1.e, verbatim quote), energy certificate annexed to the sale contract
  (RD 390/2021 art. 17.2), residence test (LIRPF art. 9.1), treaty tie-breakers, AEAT Brexit page.
- Scope note (D-11) after the TL;DR. Links added to 15116 (Modelo 720) and 26587 (tax residency).
