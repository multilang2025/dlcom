## Corrections to the plan found while drafting

- The plan says "Valencian bonuses not verified". They are now verified on BOE and Hisenda, but the **application to non-resident heirs is still a judgement** (see points below), so the post states the statute chain and ends with a CTA.
- Fact-check file wording "DA 2a gives non-resident heirs the regional rules of the Community with the most Spanish assets" is only one of three cases. The post gives all three (deceased non-resident, deceased resident in a Community, gifts of Spanish real estate).
- Ley 26/2014 text of DA 2a covered EU/EEA residents only. Ley 11/2021 removed that limit. The plan's "2018 Tribunal Supremo ruling" was not cited: no official source was opened (STS 242/2018 of 19 Feb 2018 appears only on secondary and law-firm pages, which are not approved sources).
- The Valencian filing deadline for gifts was not confirmed on ATV, so the Modelo 651 deadline was removed from the post.

2. **DA 2a case 1 (deceased not resident in Spain).** The post says the heirs apply the rules of the Community with the greatest value of Spanish assets, and the heir's own Community if there are none. Confirm this reading, including for a Spanish-resident heir of a UK-resident deceased with no Spanish assets (the heir's Valencian rules).

5. **Group I and II reductions and the home reduction** (art 10.1.a and 10.1.c) as shown in the table, and the statement that step-children are group III.
