## 1. What was announced on 5 October 2026, and by which instrument

- **Instrument: a press note (nota informativa) of the Gabinete de Prensa of the Ministerio de Hacienda, dated 5 October 2026.
  It is NOT a norm.** Title: "Previsión de modificación de los plazos de aplicación de las obligaciones pendientes para los
  empresarios y profesionales derivadas del Reglamento de sistemas informáticos de facturación (Real Decreto 1007/2023) y
  convergencia con el sistema de factura electrónica".
  Source (PDF opened): https://www.hacienda.gob.es/sgt/gabsehacienda/nota-informativa-verifactu.pdf
- Content (paraphrase, the note says): Hacienda considers it advisable to align the implementation calendars of the
  e-invoicing rules for SMEs and the self-employed and of the Reglamento de sistemas informáticos de facturación (RD 1007/2023), and
  therefore to postpone "las obligaciones pendientes de aplicación del Real Decreto 1007/2023, de 5 de diciembre, hasta
  octubre de 2028", to coincide with the e-invoicing obligation for entrepreneurs and professionals whose annual turnover does not
  exceed 8 million euros. "Una vez aprobada dicha alineación" a further modification will address convergence of the scope and
  some technical aspects, taking into account the future ViDA digital reporting requirements. The requirements of RD 1007/2023 on
  integrity, retention, accessibility, legibility, traceability and inalterability, and the other technical guarantees, "se
  mantendrán en términos sustancialmente equivalentes".
- **No BOE norm yet.** Checked: BOE summaries of 5, 6 and 7 October 2026 (API sumario): the only invoicing item is Orden
  HAC/1028/2026 (5 Oct). The BOE of 7 Oct carries RDL 28/2026 and RDL 29/2026 (housing, 6 Oct), neither mentions RD 1007/2023 or
  facturación. The Consejo de Ministros reference of 6 Oct 2026 (La Moncloa) has no Verifactu item. The consolidated RD 1007/2023
  on the BOE ("Última actualización, publicada el 03/12/2025") still reads DF 4: IS taxpayers "antes del 1 de enero de 2027", the rest
  "antes del 1 de julio de 2027" (as amended by RDL 15/2025, BOE-A-2025-24446, validated 11 Dec 2025).
- **AEAT sede (page updated 07/10/2026)**: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu.html
  carries two items: (a) "Comunicación del Ministerio de Hacienda de 05/10/2026: Previsión de ampliación del plazo de adaptación
  de los sistemas informáticos de facturación (SIF)" (links the PDF above); (b) the AEAT "NOTA INFORMATIVA: Ampliación del plazo de
  adaptación de los SIF" https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu/nota-informativa-ampliacion-plazo-adaptacion-facturacion.html
  which, on 7 Oct 2026, still states the approved dates: IS filers before 1 Jan 2027, the rest before 1 Jul 2027 (RDL 15/2025).
- **Conclusion: on 7 October 2026 the legally applicable Verifactu dates are still 1 January 2027 (companies subject to corporate
  income tax, art. 3.1.a RD 1007/2023) and 1 July 2027 (other obligated persons). The move to October 2028 is announced
  (previsión) by Hacienda; the legal text (a modification of RD 1007/2023 DF 4, instrument not named in the note, a royal
  decree-law is suggested by the press only) is not published.** Posts must say so. Re-check due: see section 5.
- The press (El Español, elderecho.com and others, 5 to 6 Oct) counts this as the second, third or fourth postponement: counts
  differ, so posts do not number it.

## 2. What stays in force (checked)

- RD 1007/2023 itself, with the guarantees on integrity, retention, accessibility, legibility, traceability and inalterability
  (Hacienda note: maintained in substantially equivalent terms). A later modification of scope and technical aspects is announced
  but not drafted publicly.
- LGT art. 29.2.j and art. 201 bis (tax offence for manufacturing, producing and marketing, and for holding, non-compliant
  invoicing software; fixed fines) are in the consolidated LGT (BOE-A-2003-23186). The note does not touch them. Whether and how they
  bite before the compliance date is not stated: not asserted in posts.
- Software producers: DF 4 RD 1007/2023 gave them nine months from the entry into force of the ministerial order (Orden HAC/1177/2024,
  in force 29 Oct 2024), that is until 29 July 2025. Not mentioned in the 5 Oct note: no change announced.
- Voluntary VERI*FACTU mode and the AEAT free app: the AEAT sede keeps them listed (page updated 7 Oct 2026). The AEAT note
  says that before the deadline it is a test period in which one may stop sending test records and invoice with another SIF.
- TicketBAI and regional systems, SII: not addressed in the note, so no change announced. (SII users are outside Verifactu per the
  AEAT FAQ, already in the FR/RU posts.)

## 3. E-invoicing order: Orden HAC/1028/2026 (opened on the BOE)

- Orden HAC/1028/2026, de 2 de octubre, "por la que se regula la solución pública de facturación electrónica", BOE núm. 247 of
  5 October 2026, BOE-A-2026-20587, https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20587 . **Disposición final única: entra
  en vigor "el día siguiente al de su publicación", so on 6 October 2026**, "dándose inicio al cómputo de los plazos previstos en
  la disposición final octava de la Ley 18/2022 ... y en las disposiciones transitorias primera a tercera y disposición final
  cuarta, del Real Decreto 238/2026". It also says the public solution must be available to obligated persons at least two months
  before the first effective application.
- Real Decreto 238/2026, de 25 de marzo (BOE 31 Mar 2026, BOE-A-2026-7295, in force 20 Apr 2026), DF 4.1, verbatim: effective
  application "contados desde la entrada en vigor de la orden ministerial": "a) Doce meses después, para los empresarios y
  profesionales cuyo volumen de operaciones ... haya excedido de 8 millones de euros durante el año natural inmediato anterior.
  b) Veinticuatro meses después, para el resto de los empresarios y profesionales."
  Computed from 6 Oct 2026: **6 October 2027 (turnover above EUR 8 million) and 6 October 2028 (everyone else)**. The BOE
  texts do not print these two calendar dates, they are the computation (posts: "octobre 2027" / "octobre 2028", "12 and 24 months
  after 6 October 2026").
- DF 4.2: for private invoice-exchange platforms, the copy-to-public-solution, interconnection and operator requirements apply 12
  months after the order enters into force (6 Oct 2027).
- Transitional provisions: first (public-sector subcontractors, 24 months); second (for those above EUR 8 million, a PDF alongside
  the electronic invoice during the first 12 months from the effective date, unless the recipient accepts the original format);
  third (invoice-status reporting voluntary for natural persons and entidades en atribución de rentas under EUR 8 million until 12
  months after their effective date).
- Exemptions, art. 4: simplified invoices (art. 4 RD 1619/2012) unless qualified simplified invoices (art. 7.2); the Ministry of
  Economy may exclude other operations by order. Scope art. 3: B2B invoices where the recipient is established in Spain.
- System, art. 5 and 11: private platforms and the AEAT public solution (UBL syntax), which is also the universal repository.
- **Interaction with Verifactu**: the order's annex lets an e-invoice carry the VERI*FACTU or TICKETBAI QR (BT-123). Hacienda's
  5 Oct note is the only statement on alignment of the calendars: Verifactu pending obligations are to be moved to October 2028,
  the date for the "SME and self-employed" e-invoicing group (turnover up to EUR 8 million). The note does not state a separate
  date for companies above EUR 8 million nor for IS taxpayers: not asserted in posts.
